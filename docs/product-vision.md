# Under the Lights: Product Vision

This document preserves the strongest ideas for taking Under the Lights beyond a prediction interface and turning it into a weekly football ritual.

These ideas are intentionally parked for a later phase. The current product priority is defining how the featured match is discovered, evaluated and selected each weekend.

## Product principle

The next level will come from ritual, emotion and memory, not from adding more decoration. Every feature should strengthen at least one of these outcomes:

- Make the weekly match feel important.
- Encourage players to discover football outside their usual leagues.
- Turn predictions and achievements into memorable stories.
- Give players reasons to return and share their journey.

## Experience ideas

### 1. The Weekly Reveal

Each featured match is unveiled like a major event. The sequence could begin in darkness, reveal the country and competition, switch on the floodlights, then introduce the two teams and the stakes.

The sequence should remain short, optional after the first viewing and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

### 2. A living world map

A dark world map records the season's journey. Every featured match adds a new illuminated destination, allowing players to see where the game has taken the community.

The map could also provide access to previous weeks, results, badges and stories from each location.

### 3. Bespoke match posters

Every featured fixture receives a collectible poster using the clubs, stadium or city, team colours, competition, date and editorial stakes.

These posters can become the visual identity of each week and the foundation for social sharing.

### 4. The resolution moment

After the final whistle, the player's prediction becomes a result ticket. Score, goalscorer, timing and bonus calls are resolved one after another before the total points and unlocked achievements are revealed.

This should feel like opening the result of a weekly challenge rather than reading a standard score summary.

### 5. A spectacular Trophy Room

Achievements become collectible objects rather than ordinary cards. Each badge can include its rarity, unlock date and the match where it was earned.

Opening a badge should reveal its story and make rare accomplishments feel genuinely valuable.

### 6. A stronger player identity

Every member can have a shareable competitor card featuring their world rank, accuracy, current streak, countries explored, rarest achievements and prediction style.

### 7. Live match mode

During the selected fixture, the page can switch to a live experience with a match timeline, important events, the current state of the player's prediction and the points still available.

The goal is not to replace a live-score service. It is to make the selected match and the player's choices feel alive.

### 8. Optional sound design

Subtle sounds could support key moments: floodlights switching on, a distant crowd during the reveal and a short impact when an achievement is unlocked.

Sound must remain optional, muted by default and compatible with accessibility preferences.

### 9. Automatic social cards

After each result, players can generate a card containing their score, match, ranking movement and unlocked badges. The format should be instantly understandable and designed for social platforms.

## Suggested implementation order

1. The Weekly Reveal
2. The resolution moment
3. The living world map
4. Bespoke match posters
5. The Trophy Room
6. Player identity cards
7. Automatic social cards
8. Live match mode
9. Optional sound design

## Current product question

Before building these ideas, Under the Lights needs a reliable answer to one foundational question:

> How do we find and select the one fixture that deserves the spotlight each weekend?

The selection system must surface meaningful matches across the full Soccerverse world, remain explainable to players and avoid defaulting to the largest or most familiar competitions.
